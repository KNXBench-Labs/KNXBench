Preflight: confirmed no host `knx`/`knx-server` process; one IP-only `knx bus discover` found exactly one tunnelling gateway (no endpoint copied to tracked files). This proves current presence and mask, not the physical model or storage coverage. No programming-mode search, address change or download was sent. No firewall change or key use.
