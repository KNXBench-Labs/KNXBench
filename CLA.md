# KNXBench Individual Contributor License Agreement

Version 1.0, 2026-09-30

Thank you for contributing to KNXBench. This agreement explains which rights you grant
when you contribute, and which promises the maintainer makes to you in return. It
exists for one reason, which is explained in plain words first, and then made binding
in the numbered sections.

## Why this agreement exists

KNXBench is published under the GNU Affero General Public License, version 3 or later
(`AGPL-3.0-or-later`). Everyone may use KNXBench for any purpose, commercial work
included: planning and commissioning customer installations, running it inside a
company, charging for the engineering done with it.

What the project does not want is a closed product built on KNXBench and sold without
giving anything back. The AGPL already requires that anyone who distributes a modified
KNXBench, or offers it to users over a network, releases the complete source under the
AGPL. A vendor who wants to ship a proprietary product based on KNXBench instead needs a
separate commercial license from the maintainer.

The maintainer can only offer that commercial license if he holds the necessary rights
to the whole code base, including your contribution. This agreement grants those rights.
In return, section 4 promises that your contribution will always stay available as free
software under the AGPL.

This agreement does **not** transfer your copyright. You remain the author and owner of
your contribution and may use it in any other way you like.
