Date: 26 October 2026. Exact application `M-0001_A-9205-04-9270`, mask
`0025h`. This research distinguishes source declarations, a reporter's field
observation and an experimental interpretation. It is not hardware acceptance.

## Public field observation

The [reporter's comment](https://github.com/KNXBench-Labs/KNXBench/issues/30#issuecomment-6410767067)
of 26 October 2026 supplies a private-journal diagnostic excerpt from build
`0.1.0-alpha.16+g718daf9`:

- Object 1, PID 7: one element, two octets, raw hexadecimal `0116`.
- The diagnostic reports `mutation_may_have_started: false`.
- The reporter states that the device continues to function normally.

Only that published excerpt was inspected; the full journal and physical bus
were not independently examined. Both diagnostic commits `a15b8f0eb` and
`5f1ad454e` are ancestors of the stated build. The big-endian interpretation of
`0116` agrees with the public source's object-1 absolute/task address `0116h`.
This does not establish the replies of objects 2/3, a property-description PDT,
other firmware or full recovery.

The same attempt started a new operation and did not show the earlier busy
banner. This observation does not establish the cause or permanent resolution
of the earlier start failure. The nullable early-admission `refusal` is separate
from a failure inside an existing operation journal.

## Normative boundary and intended interpretation

Original **Resources `03_05_01` v01.10.01**, section 4.2.7, printed page 31,
was re-read in the authorised local PDF. It defines a four-octet absolute
pointer, `PDT_UNSIGNED_LONG`, and the pointer's allocation/unload semantics.
The public master's two permitted datatypes do not amend that clause or prove
that every device implements both. The earlier
[source-semantics investigation](2026-10-16-system2-source-semantics.md)
correctly kept resource length distinct from wire width.

A targeted experimental model can preserve either a single two-octet or a
single four-octet answer without inventing raw padding. A numeric comparison
against an independently retained source address is distinct from preserving
the raw representation. Admitting the two-octet representation on objects 2/3
remains a source-bound hypothesis, not a field result. Such a policy must be
explicitly versioned and bound to the exact configured application, with old
operations retaining their original policy.

This evidence does not relax hardware identity, durable pre-write evidence,
consent, final memory read-back, Loaded-state or written-application checks.
It does not resolve survival of masked/unwritten bytes across Unload, allocator
history, task reconstruction or physical original-state recovery. Production
implementation and test results must be recorded separately after execution.
