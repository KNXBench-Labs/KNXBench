# Source-ID ledger

The status of record for every tracked source ID ([ADR-0076](../adr/0076-one-ledger-is-the-status-of-record.md)).
A status written anywhere else is history or an error. Evidence stays in the
owners' dossiers: [ALPHA_READINESS](https://github.com/KNXBench-Labs/KNXBench/blob/bca2d3336b96/docs/archive/alpha-0.1/ALPHA_READINESS.md) (alpha packages),
[COMMISSIONING_ALPHA_LEDGER](https://github.com/KNXBench-Labs/KNXBench/blob/bca2d3336b96/docs/archive/alpha-0.1/COMMISSIONING_ALPHA_LEDGER.md) (commissioning:
evidence, fallback, unblock) and [UI_ALPHA_READINESS](https://github.com/KNXBench-Labs/KNXBench/blob/bca2d3336b96/docs/archive/alpha-0.1/UI_ALPHA_READINESS.md)
(UI), removed from the tree on 2026-10-08 together with the finished
[alpha-release-goal.md](https://github.com/KNXBench-Labs/KNXBench/blob/bca2d3336b96/docs/archive/alpha-0.1/alpha-release-goal.md). What is still
open is summarized in [OPEN_WORK](../OPEN_WORK.md); removed files are listed in
[REMOVED_DOCS](../history/REMOVED_DOCS.md).

## How to update

- **Owner edits its rows.** The session named in *Owner* changes *Status*,
  *Owner disposition* and *Evidence*. The alpha controller changes *Owner* and
  *Route*. Change one row per ID; do not copy a status into another file.
- **Status** uses `TODO`, `IN_PROGRESS`, `DONE`, `BLOCKED_EXTERNAL`,
  `WAITING_OWNER`, `WAITING_DECISION`, `ACCEPTED_BOUNDARY`, `LATER`
  ([meaning](https://github.com/KNXBench-Labs/KNXBench/blob/bca2d3336b96/docs/archive/alpha-0.1/alpha-release-goal.md#22-work-loop)). `DONE` needs a named
  test, artifact or review and a revision in *Evidence*.
- **Owner disposition** is the owner track's own finer word, or `—`. The
  commissioning words are defined in
  [COMMISSIONING_ALPHA_LEDGER](https://github.com/KNXBench-Labs/KNXBench/blob/bca2d3336b96/docs/archive/alpha-0.1/COMMISSIONING_ALPHA_LEDGER.md#meaning-of-dispositions).
- **New findings** get a row in *Post-snapshot IDs* with their origin. The 180
  snapshot rows come from the frozen
  [OFFENE_PUNKTE](https://github.com/KNXBench-Labs/KNXBench/blob/bca2d3336b96/docs/archive/OFFENE_PUNKTE.md) inventory; their IDs and
  priorities never change here.
- **Owner values:** `alpha` (an AR package or a recorded boundary in
  alpha-release-goal), `ui` (`goal-ui.md`), `commission` (`goal-commission.md`),
  `later` (separate scope, not an alpha task). Since 2026-10-09 every
  non-terminal row is owned by `later`; `alpha`, `ui` and `commission` remain only
  on finished rows as history, and their goal-file routes are historical.
- **Recount** after an edit; the counts below are part of the file.
  `cargo run -p xtask -- check-ledger` checks the rows, the counts and that no
  other document carries a per-ID status; on a stale count it prints the
  expected line.
